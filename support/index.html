Your Klyra account

What is a Klyra account?

One login for all Klyra apps. You sign in with your email and a password. Your data is encrypted with keys made from that password on your device, so the server only holds scrambled data.

You can manage your account at klyra.cc/account: change your password, sign out, or delete your account.

Do I need an account?

No. Every app works as a guest: choose "Continue as guest". Your data then stays on that device only and isn't backed up. If you sign in later, your guest data is added to your account.

An account gives you sync between devices and a copy that survives losing your phone.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder first. Then try to create the account again after a few minutes. If it still doesn't arrive, write to support@klyra.cc from the address you signed up with.

After you open the link in the email, you land on the account page. Then sign in. Your recovery key is shown at your first sign-in.

Forgot your password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen of any app, or on the account page. Type your email and we send you a link. Open it, then enter your recovery key and choose a new password.

You need the recovery key. Your data is locked with your password. Without your password and your recovery key, nobody can restore your encrypted data, not even us.

Your recovery key

It starts with KNR1- and is shown once, when you create your account. We never see it and can't make a new one for you.

  • Save it in a password manager, or print it and keep it somewhere safe.
  • You need it only if you forget your password.
  • Keep it private. With the key and a reset email, someone could set a new password for your account.

Delete your account

In any Klyra app, open Me and look under Account and privacy for Delete account. You can also do it on the account page.

  1. Enter your password.
  2. Type this phrase: Delete my data from all klyra apps (capital letters don't matter).
  3. Tap the delete button. It stays off until both are right.

This can't be undone. One account is shared by all Klyra apps, so deleting it erases your data from all of them, on every device. Your recovery key won't bring it back.

Sync and offline

  • Your data is saved on your device first, so the apps work without a connection.
  • When you are online and signed in, changes are synced in the background: about once a minute, when you come back to the app, and after you make a change.
  • If the app says changes are waiting on the phone, they will sync when you are back online. Look under Me for the sync row in apps that have one.
  • Guest data is never synced. It stays on the device until you sign in.

Android: install and update

  • Our Android apps are downloaded from each app's own website, not from an app store. Each app page has a download button and install steps.
  • Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser. That's expected for apps outside a store.
  • Updates: the app checks its own website for a new version and tells you inside the app. Tap the message to download and install it.
  • If Android says the update can't be installed over the old app, or a page says the signing key changed, uninstall the old version and install the new one. Sign in first and let it sync, because guest data on the device is removed when you uninstall.

Report a bug

Email support@klyra.cc and include:

  • Which app, and its version (Android or web).
  • Your device and Android version, or your browser.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  • Whether you were signed in or a guest, and whether you were online.

Never send your password or your recovery key. We will never ask for them.
