Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This page explains what happens to your data in Klyra apps and on this site. It covers Kinn, Klyra Weather, Klyra Contacts and Klyra Orbit.

The short version

  • What you save in an app is encrypted on your device before it is sent anywhere. Our server stores the scrambled result and can't read it.
  • Your password is never sent to us. It is turned into keys on your device.
  • Signing in is optional. As a guest, your data stays on your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers on this site or in the apps.
  • We don't sell data. We don't have your readable data to sell.

What our server stores

If you create a Klyra account, the server (Supabase) holds:

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in and to send account emails.
A login secret made from your passwordNot your password. Your device derives it, and the login service stores only a protected form of it.
Password-stretching settings (a salt and cost numbers)So any of your devices can turn your password into the same keys.
Your master key, locked twiceOne copy is locked with a key from your password, one with your recovery key. Without those, it is random-looking bytes.
Your encrypted recordsWhat each app saves for you, as encrypted blobs of data with a random id, a version number and the date of the last change.
TimestampsWhen your account and keys were created or changed.

The server also knows which Klyra apps have stored something for you, because each app keeps its records in its own place. It knows roughly how much you store, and when you last synced.

What our server can't read

  • Your password and your recovery key.
  • The content of your records: your todos, habits, goals and feelings in Kinn, your saved places in Klyra Weather, your contacts in Klyra Contacts.
  • Your display name. It is saved inside an encrypted record.
  • Guest data. It never leaves your device.

This also means we can't reset your password or restore your data for you. If you lose both your password and your recovery key, the data is gone.

Klyra Orbit

Orbit shares live location between people who invite each other. It is designed so the server sees as little as possible: random ids, hashes of access codes, encrypted messages of a few fixed sizes, and the times they were written. There is no list of who knows whom on the server, and no names or locations in readable form. The server does learn that your account uses the sharing service and when it makes calls.

What stays on your device

  • Your unlocked key. On Android it is kept in the system's secure storage so the app opens without your password. On the web it is kept in memory only, so you sign in again after closing the tab.
  • A copy of your records, encrypted, so the apps work offline.
  • In Klyra Contacts, your call log. It is not synced.

This website

This site is plain pages with no scripts, no cookies, no analytics and no requests to other sites. Its font is served from the site itself. If you open a link to another site or to an app, that site's own rules apply.

Your control

  • Use a guest. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Delete your account. In any app under Me, Account and privacy, or on the account page. It deletes your data from all Klyra apps. See Support.
  • Ask us. Write to support@klyra.cc or contact@klyra.cc.
