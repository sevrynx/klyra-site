Private by design

  • End-to-end encrypted. Your data is locked on your device. The server stores scrambled data and can't read it.
  • Your password stays with you. It is turned into keys on your device. The real password never reaches our server.
  • One recovery key. You get it when you create your account. Without your password and that key, nobody can restore your data, not even us.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers on this site or in the apps.
